Common questions

I scored higher than someone above me on the leaderboard.
The board sorts by score descending, then by submission time ascending — so ties go to whoever got there first.

My score didn't post.
The end-of-run banner shows the status. If it says ⚠ couldn't post, tap it to retry. Network blips can drop the first attempt.

I want to change my name.
Open the home screen → tap your identity row → set a new name. Old leaderboard rows update automatically.

I want my data removed.
Email me with your player number or display name. I'll delete your rows.

How do I unlock the dark theme?
Reach 16384 points in any mode. The first unlock (Forest) comes at 1024 points, Ocean at 4096.

Will there be ads or in-app purchases?
No. Stack & Merge is free and stays free. No paywalls, no ads, no IAPs.

Privacy

See the privacy policy — short version: a random device identifier and anonymous gameplay events. No tracking, no advertising, no real names unless you choose one.
